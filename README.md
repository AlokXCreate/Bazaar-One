# Bazaar One - Intelligent Unified Indian Marketplace

Bazaar One is an intelligent digital marketplace platform designed to bridge traditional Indian local markets (kiranas, vegetable and spice vendors, regional artisans) with modern retail enterprises.

## Key Highlights
- Dual-mode Marketplace: Traditional Mandi / Bazaar and Modern Hypermarket views
- Smart AI Shopping Planner (Daily, Weekly, Monthly grocery budgeting & auto-lists)
- Vendor Management & Real-time Inventory tracking
- Multi-role Portal: Customer, Merchant, Delivery Partner, Platform Admin
- Integrated Billing & Document Engine (PDF, Word DOCX, JSON, HTML)
- Location Discovery & Interactive Shop Locator
- Secure Digital Payments (UPI QR, Intent, Cards, COD)
